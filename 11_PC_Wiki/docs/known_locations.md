# Known Locations

A quick reference to the current investigation, the party's home base, and major places from earlier adventures. See the [session recaps](session_recaps/session_001.md) for detailed routes, rooms, and encounters.

## Gray Hollow — Current Arc

### Gray Hollow

A town isolated by supernatural mist. Trade has nearly stopped, food is running short, and residents who try to leave describe losing their way or returning with gaps in their memories. Outside the walls, the party felt pressure to flee; inside, that feeling became unnaturally reassuring.

The party defeated mist-born Wardens and a spectral dragon at the gate before being admitted. Unexplained bell sounds and restricted access to the old temple are the current leads.

### The Muffled Bell

Hessa Brant's inn and the party's current lodging. The group recovered here after the gate battle, and Elaris donated provisions to help with the shortages. It is a useful place to speak with residents and regroup.

### Gray Hollow Temple

An old temple with restricted entry, two guards, and faintly glowing ward lines. Accounts from Tomas Vale and Captain Orla Fen drew the party's attention here.

**Current position:** Outside the temple entrance. The party has not yet entered or established what lies inside.

## Everlund — Home Base

### Everlund

The city where the party first met Elaris and returns between expeditions for supplies, shopping, and crafting.

[Everlund Shops](everlund_shops.md) · [Resonant Crafting](crafting.md)

### Moongleam Tower

Elaris's base in Everlund, where the party has received lodging, briefings, healing, and crafting assistance. In his laboratory, they witnessed the recovered Farstep fragment join his gathered crystalline core before the Gray Hollow expedition.

## Earlier Adventures

### Silent Coin Tavern

A haunted roadside tavern in the Moonwood, bound to the possessive Coiled Coin. The party reunited its four shards, defeated the final guardian, and escaped through a hidden river passage as the building collapsed. Three days passed outside during their short time within.

### Brindleford

A battered settlement near Redmark Keep. The party found shelter at **the Bent Wheel** and learned of three approaches to the keep. They chose the Hunter Trail and located the forgotten drainage culvert.

### Redmark Keep

The ruined stronghold connected to Red Banner activity and attacks near Brindleford. The party entered through the drainage culvert, defeated the Redmaw Tyrant at the War Altar, recovered the red-gold gemstone fragment, and escaped the failing command vault.

### Waymark Crossing

A mining town supported by Farstep Works. The party fought off raiders at the Lower Road, Supply Shed, and **Mercy House**, then entered the mine to rescue those trapped below.

### Farstep Works

A crystal mine whose passages had begun shifting and looping. The party rescued miners and prisoners, defeated the raider headquarters and the Rift Widow, and recovered the gemstone fragment. With it contained, the impossible routes began to settle, allowing the party to return through Waymark to Everlund.
