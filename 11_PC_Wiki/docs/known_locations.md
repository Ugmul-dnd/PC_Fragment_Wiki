## Everlund

The city where the party met Elaris Vaunthel and received the first mission of the campaign.

Known market notes: [Everlund Shops](everlund_shops.md)

## Moongleam Tower

Elaris Vaunthel's tower in Everlund. The party received lodging and mission briefings there, returned after escaping the Silent Coin Tavern, and left the recovered Coiled Coin in Elaris's care for controlled study.

## Silent Coin Tavern

An abandoned roadside tavern in the Moonwood. It was aware, rule-bound, and dangerous. Once the party entered, the outside world vanished from sight and sound.

The party recovered the four pieces of the Coiled Coin, defeated the tavern's final guardian, and escaped through a hidden river passage as the building collapsed. Three days passed outside while the party experienced only a few hours within.

A battered settlement near Redmark Keep. The party reached it after crossing an open battlefield between gnolls and Red Banner forces. Its caravans are damaged, supplies are limited, and its defenders are struggling to keep the roads open.

The party found shelter and a safe long rest at **The Bent Wheel**. Local contacts described three approaches to Redmark Keep: the exposed Old North Road, the concealed Field Ditch Route, and the western Hunter Trail.

The party chose the Hunter Trail and successfully crossed it. They found an abandoned windmill on the ridge, then used the high ground to locate Redmark's forgotten Drainage Culvert.

## The Bent Wheel

A quiet Brindleford inn run by locals who have watched the siege tighten around town. Old Brenn shares his knowledge beside its hearth. Leonidus found Rhaegar staying upstairs, and the party completed a safe long rest here.

## Hunter Trail

An old path climbing the western ridge toward Redmark Keep. The party crossed its thorn ledges, loose shale, and falling-stone hazards, then fought three ogres attacking an abandoned windmill. From the ridge, they observed Redmark's western defenses and located the old runoff cut.

## Redmark Drainage Culvert

A forgotten lower-water route beneath Redmark's western wall. The party opened its root-choked gate without alerting the surface patrols, crossed a sluice basin occupied by gelatinous cubes, and fought through a corpse-filled maintenance gallery.

The lower works connect to Redmark's old command infrastructure. A three-valve pressure mechanism opened the route to a broken service rise leading upward into the keep.

## Redmark Keep

The party's Arc 2 destination: an old ruin near Brindleford connected to caravan attacks, missing supplies, gnoll violence, and disciplined Red Banner activity.

The party entered the keep through its forgotten Drainage Culvert, bypassed the guarded western gate, and defeated the Redmaw Tyrant at a damaged War Altar. They freed the red-gold gemstone fragment trapped there and escaped through an eastern drainage route as the command vault began to fail.

## Waymark Crossing

A mine-dependent town built around the Farstep Works. When the party arrived, empty ore carts, injured travelers, hungry residents, and fleeing groups showed how badly the mine's failure had damaged the town.

Waymark was under attack by organized raiders marked with ash-white paint around one eye. The party saved the Lower Road and Supply Shed and is continuing uphill toward the mine.

## Waymark Supply Shed

A store of rope, lamps, oil, climbing equipment, and other tools needed by Farstep Works. Raiders were preparing oil, pitch, smoke, black powder, and demolition materials here until the party stopped them.

## Mercy House

Waymark's house of healing, located on the road toward Farstep Works. Townsfolk believe survivors and healers may be sheltering there. The party has been directed toward it but has not arrived yet.

## Farstep Works

The crystal mine that supports Waymark Crossing. Its routes have begun shifting, looping, and leading miners somewhere other than expected. Elaris suspects the disturbance may be connected to movement and space.
