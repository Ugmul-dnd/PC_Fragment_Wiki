## Current Table Position

**Arc 4 — Gray Hollow.** The party is **level 6** and has survived the fall of the town's temple wards. Elaris freed the fragment from the chapel altar but was gravely wounded in the resulting magical backlash. Wazeau's *Daylight* revealed the monsters gathering in the darkness, and the party escaped back to **the Muffled Bell**.

The inn was soon besieged by corrupted ward-creatures. During the fight, **Thalira**, a mysterious figure from Elaris's past, arrived to escort him to safety with the freshly recovered fragment. **Aiaia pursued and confronted her directly**, returning with a warning and more questions. The previously gathered combined core was **not** taken with them.

**Where we stopped:** The group has secured the battered inn for the night. The next session opens **the following morning**, with Gray Hollow's streets darkened and its temple in ruins.
