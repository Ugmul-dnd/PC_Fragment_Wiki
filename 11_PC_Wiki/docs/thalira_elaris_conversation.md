# Elaris and Thalira — Old Friends, Unfinished Wounds

*A recap of the conversation overheard during Session 15.*

[← Back to Session 015](session_recaps/session_015.md)

## An unexpected reunion

The party found Elaris injured but conscious in a clean, lived-in chamber deep beneath Gray Hollow. Thalira had tended his wounds, brought him water, and made sure he could rest. They spoke with the familiarity of old friends—teasing each other gently about how little their habits had changed.

A dark, newly extracted fragment hung suspended over a stone workstation, held in a web of pale light. It gave off none of its former glow.

## Why Elaris is searching

Thalira asked when Elaris began looking for fragments, and how many he had found. He admitted that he is deliberately searching now, but he **would not tell her the number or where they are kept**.

Elaris said that when fragments joined at the Silent Coin he was **not there**, but **felt** something happen: pieces rejoining, something wounded beginning to heal. He believes the fragments cause suffering to the places and people around them. He intends to gather them, ease that harm, and ultimately bring them together.

Thalira believed he had felt something real. She did not agree that he understood where that course would lead. What frightened her was how far he might be willing to go.

## A shared history

As Elaris looked around the sanctuary, he realized how long Thalira had lived there, quietly caring for the place and watching Gray Hollow change above her. He hadn't known she had made a home beneath the town. **“Still the Warden,”** he remarked—a familiar acknowledgment that she had never stopped protecting what she could. Thalira answered that she wouldn't know how to stop. When Elaris recalled how seriously she had always taken their promise, she gently reminded him: **“We both did.”**

Their shared past soon became harder to avoid. Thalira wanted to know how much Elaris had told the adventurers who were following him. He admitted he had told them only part of his story—and **had never mentioned that he and Thalira had once worked together**, or how far back their history went.

Thalira urged him to be honest with his companions. Elaris struggled with where to begin, and her answer was simple: **“With what you remember.”** He understood what she was asking, but could not yet bring himself to say more.

## The disagreement over the stone

Elaris asked whether the sealed Gray Hollow fragment could still feel the others. Thalira said she did not know. He asked if she would let him take it. She answered **no**.

He still meant to continue helping the places touched by fragments. She wished he would remain long enough for them to talk. Neither shouted. They sounded like friends who still cared deeply, yet could not agree.

## The warning

Thalira discovered the party was listening. She took the sealed fragment, armed herself, and warned that Elaris—and the world—would be safer if he stayed. She told the party to abandon the quest and leave her home. A luminous, armored guardian appeared between her and the entrance.

**The session ended before initiative.**

## Questions left open

What happened when Elaris and Thalira worked together? What promise do they remember? What has Elaris not yet told the party? Can the fragments truly heal the world's wounds—and at what risk?
