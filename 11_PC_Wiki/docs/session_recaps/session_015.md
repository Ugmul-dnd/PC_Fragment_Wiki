# Session 015 — Beneath the Bells

Morning returned to Gray Hollow without the oppressive mist. At the **Muffled Bell**, the party checked on Tovin and found the warded case holding the gathered fragment core safe. They took the portable case with them and followed Thalira's scorched trail back to the ruined temple. Brother Caldus was shaken: the old sense of protection around the chapel had vanished.

A hidden passage below the altar led into a much older complex, lined with shields, open hands, and worn wards. Two sets of footprints showed **Elaris walking beside Thalira**, injured but not a prisoner being dragged along. The path opened into a vault of great hanging bells, where **two Hook Horrors and a Cloaker** attacked. Professor Squanchius was swallowed up in the Cloaker's embrace and nearly lost, until his companions got him back on his feet. The wizard had a few fiery surprises of his own.

Deeper in the Hollow, ancient shelter rooms and binding circles suggested the builders had once protected people and contained dangers. The party found an old chest wrapped in unnatural roots. Fire and magic disturbed **two Shambling Mounds**, which managed to engulf more than one unfortunate adventurer before falling. The cache held **a Luckstone, a pair of Sending Stones, and two Greater Healing Potions**.

Two great **Warden Guardians** barred the last gateway. They struck with shields and waves of force, but focused attacks and Aiaia's **Hunger of Hadar** helped the party break through. A Resonant Remnant and a mysterious magical rope were recovered. Beyond the broken constructs, the party entered something unexpected: clean halls, fresh supplies, books, and carefully maintained living quarters. Someone called this ancient place home.

At last, they heard **Elaris and Thalira** talking. The two spoke like old friends with a painful disagreement. Elaris believes gathering fragments and reuniting them can heal the suffering around them; Thalira believes he is risking too much. She had tended his wounds but refused to hand over the newly freed Gray Hollow fragment. More unsettlingly, she urged him to tell his companions more about **their shared past**.

Then Thalira noticed they were listening. Taking the sealed stone into her cloak, she drew her weapons and ordered the party to abandon the quest. A shining armored guardian formed in front of her.

**Where we stopped:** The guardian has appeared; **initiative has not been rolled**. Elaris remains injured behind Thalira, and the confrontation awaits.
