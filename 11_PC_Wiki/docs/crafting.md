# Resonant Crafting

Resonant Remnants are fragment-touched materials that can create magic items or improve equipment you already own. Keep a favorite weapon, strengthen a magical focus, or improve an item's healing power as you adventure.

Crafting requires a suitable item, compatible materials, and an appropriate crafter or process. Agree on the finished upgrade with the DM before spending Remnants. Time, access, and any additional costs depend on the project.

## How Investment Works

Equipment improvements have separate costs:

- **Core enchantment:** The item's normal progression, such as a +1 weapon becoming +2.
- **Augmentation:** Additional weapon damage, spell damage, or an improved healing die.
- **Uncapping:** Removes a damage augmentation's once-per-turn limit.

Investment in one does not pay for another. Upgrading the core preserves purchased augmentations, and upgrading an augmentation preserves purchased uncapping.

## Core Enchantments

| Core tier | Total core investment | Cost from previous tier |
|---|---:|---:|
| Uncommon / approved +1 item | 1 Remnant | 1 |
| Rare / approved +2 item | 3 Remnants | 2 |
| Very Rare / approved +3 item | 5 Remnants | 2 |
| Legendary | Story milestone | Ask the DM |

An existing eligible magic item starts with credit for its current core tier. **Upgrading a +1 weapon to +2 costs 2 Remnants**, rather than paying the full 3 again.

These are approved item progressions. Not every item can gain a generic +1, +2, or +3 bonus.

## Weapon Damage Augmentations

Add one extra damage die to a weapon. Choose its damage type with the DM when crafting.

| Extra damage | Total augmentation cost | Cost from previous step |
|---|---:|---:|
| +1d6 | 1 Remnant | 1 |
| +1d8 | 2 Remnants | 1 |
| +1d10 | 3 Remnants | 1 |

**Once per turn**, when you hit a creature with the weapon, you can deal its extra damage to that creature. This requires no separate action.

- Each weapon can have **only one Resonant damage rider**.
- Each weapon has its own limit. Two separately augmented weapons can each activate on the same turn.
- A reaction attack on another creature's turn can activate the rider again, even if you used it on your own turn.
- A larger die replaces the previous die.

### Uncapping a Weapon

Spend **3 additional Remnants per weapon** to apply its rider on **every successful hit**.

Purchase uncapping once. It remains unlocked if you later increase the rider die.

| Rider | Die investment | Uncapping | Total augmentation investment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| +1d6 on every hit | 1 | 3 | 4 |
| +1d8 on every hit | 2 | 3 | 5 |
| +1d10 on every hit | 3 | 3 | 6 |

These totals do not include the weapon's core enchantment.

## Healing Augmentations

Improve an item's existing additional healing die.

| Replacement healing die | Total augmentation cost | Cost from previous step |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1d6 | 1 Remnant | 1 |
| 1d8 | 2 Remnants | 1 |
| 1d10 | 3 Remnants | 1 |

The purchased die replaces the original additional healing die. Its trigger, eligible targets, and frequency remain unchanged.

**Example:** A +1 Moon Sickle's additional 1d4 healing becomes 1d6 for 1 Remnant. Its other features and bonuses remain intact. Increasing that die to 1d8 later costs 1 more Remnant.

This progression improves an existing healing property. It does not give an item a new healing ability.

## Spell Damage Augmentations

Place a spell damage augmentation on an **arcane focus or armor**. Its benefit applies while you hold the focus or wear the armor.

| Extra spell damage | Total augmentation cost | Cost from previous step |
|---|---:|---:|
| +1d6 | 1 Remnant | 1 |
| +1d8 | 2 Remnants | 1 |
| +1d10 | 3 Remnants | 1 |

**Once per turn**, when you roll damage for a spell you cast, you can add the augmentation die to **one damage roll**.

If that roll determines damage for several creatures, the extra damage applies to all of them. Roll the extra die once alongside the spell damage, then resolve each creature's saving throw, resistance, and immunity normally.

This works with cantrips, spell attacks, area spells, and ongoing spell damage. The spell does not need to have been cast that turn.

### Uncapping Spell Damage

Spend **3 additional Remnants** to apply the augmentation to **each spell damage roll**, removing its once-per-turn limit.

| Spell situation | Standard augmentation | Uncapped augmentation |
|---|---|---|
| **Fireball** | Add one die to the shared damage roll, affecting all targets | Same |
| **Eldritch Blast** | Add one die to one beam's damage roll | Add one die to every beam that hits |
| Another damaging spell on the same turn | No further use after activating that turn | Its damage roll also gains the extra die |
| **Hunger of Hadar** start- and end-of-turn damage | Choose one damage roll on that turn | Both damage rolls gain the extra die |
| Spell damage on another creature's turn | A new opportunity to activate | Each damage roll gains the extra die |

Different damage types within a single damage roll do not grant additional augmentation dice.

Each item can have only one spell damage rider, and a caster benefits from **only one spell damage augmentation at a time**. Holding, wearing, or switching multiple augmented items does not multiply activations.

Uncapping remains unlocked when the die increases. It does not change normal spellcasting or action restrictions.

## Example Projects

| Finished item | Core investment | Augmentation | Uncapping | Total investment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| +1 weapon with +1d6 once per turn | 1 | 1 | — | 2 |
| +2 weapon with +1d6 once per turn | 3 | 1 | — | 4 |
| +2 weapon with +1d6 on every hit | 3 | 1 | 3 | 7 |
| +1 Moon Sickle with additional 1d6 healing | 1 | 1 | — | 2 |
| +2 Moon Sickle with additional 1d10 healing | 3 | 3 | — | 6 |

**Example upgrade:** Your +2 weapon with a 1d6 rider represents 4 total investment. Upgrading its core to +3 costs **2 more Remnants**. The rider stays, bringing the total investment to 6.

Total investment describes the materials represented in an item, not necessarily what you must spend today. Your existing core and purchased upgrades already count.

## Planning Your Craft

Bring the DM your item, desired upgrade, and available Remnants. Together, confirm the effect, damage type where relevant, crafter, timing, and any special requirements.

The finished item's rarity and availability depend on its combined abilities. Having enough Remnants does not automatically make every upgrade available.
