# Open Questions

These are player-facing mysteries the party may want to remember.

## Silent Coin Tavern

* What remains beneath the collapsed tavern?
* What exactly was the old altar beneath the tavern built to do?
* Did removing the Coiled Coin fully stabilize the place, or only quiet it for now?

## The Coiled Coin

* What exactly is the Coiled Coin?
* Why did it turn ownership into obsession?
* Why did the old altar split it into four pieces?
* What will Elaris learn from studying it?
* Was the relic afraid before it was placed in the case?

## Elaris's Mission

* What does Elaris already know about relic fragments?
* Why did he choose this party?
* How does the Silent Coin Tavern connect to the prophecy he shared?

## Redmark Road

* What does the Red Banner want from Redmark Keep and the territory around it?
* How complete is the Red Banner's siege of Brindleford?
* Why are gnolls and the Red Banner both fighting for the same roads?
* What do the red cloth markers mean?
* Who or what is the Red Banner authority called **Command**?
* What will happen to the remaining Red Banner and gnoll forces now that the Redmaw Tyrant is dead and the fragment is gone?
* What made the payroll-and-relief detail try to use the lower waterworks when Redmark fell?

## Waymark Crossing and Farstep Works

* Why are the mine's routes shifting, looping, and connecting to the wrong places?
* What is the movement-linked relic Elaris suspects is below Farstep Works?
* Who are the ash-white-eye-marked raiders?
* Why do they want witnesses, names, records, maps, route tools, and mine access?
* What do the captured raider and the escaped attackers know?
* What happened to the people the raiders took deeper into Farstep Works?
* Who are the prisoners shown on the raiders' barracks map, and how can they be freed safely?
* What is the larger headquarters area beyond the prisoner location?
* Who was calling for help around the sabotaged Split Lift, and where are they now?
* Why are dangerous creatures appearing in the upper mine?
* How far has the mine's instability spread?

## The Deneith Brothers

* Why did Seraphine summon Leonidus and Rhaegar to the Northern Reaches?
* Where is she now?
* Why did she erase nearly every trace of her life before disappearing?
* What will Elaris's contacts discover about her?
