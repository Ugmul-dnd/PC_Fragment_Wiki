# Known NPCs

## Allies And Contacts

|Name|What The Party Knows|
|-|-|
|Elaris Vaunthel|A scholar in Everlund who invited the party to investigate strange places tied to old catastrophes, fallen stars, and relic fragments. He now holds both the sealed Coiled Coin and the red-gold Redmark fragment for study. He believes the Farstep crisis may involve movement and space, issued the party another containment case, and promised Leonidus and Rhaegar that he would investigate Seraphine's disappearance through his contacts.|
|Tovin Reed|Elaris's cheerful and capable carriage driver. He waited outside the Silent Coin Tavern for three days, later drove the party toward Waymark Crossing, and was guarding the bound raider captured at the Lower Road when the party entered the mine.|
|Caldus Venn|A healer at Waymark's Mercy House. He sheltered the party after they drove off attackers at the back door, restored their strength, and warned them that the raiders had taken people and mine supplies toward Farstep Works.|
|Marta Vennick|The steward of Farstep Works. She asked the party to enter the mine, find the trapped shift, and rescue anyone taken below.|
|Mara Vell|A Brindleford roadwarden rescued from beneath a collapsed shrine during the battlefield crossing. The party carried her through the closing gate.|
|Mira Fenwick|The practical caravan-yard boss in Brindleford. She explained the three known approaches to Redmark Keep and directed the party to other local contacts.|
|Captain Orven Hale|Brindleford's tired but disciplined watch captain. He confirmed the Red Banner's road control, the gnoll presence in the low ground, and the risks of each approach.|
|Old Brenn|An elderly local at The Bent Wheel who knows the western ridge and Redmark's history. He recommended the Hunter Trail and remembered the keep's old runoff systems.|
|Tessa Marran|Brindleford's shrine-healer. Her shrine supplied the party with three healer's kits before the Redmark approach.|
|Rescued Redmark Scavenger|A frightened scavenger rescued from an ogre-battered windmill on the Hunter Trail. He admitted stealing a Resonant Remnant from an abandoned Redmark office, described frequent patrols inside the keep, and returned toward Brindleford after the party saved him.|

## Figures Encountered In The Silent Coin Tavern

|Name / Description|What The Party Knows|
|-|-|
|Copper-Tongued Host|A ghostly or supernatural host in the common room that demanded the party sit, wait, and be served. The party defeated or banished it.|
|Keyless Shade|A shadowy guardian tied to Room 7 and the phrase "Guests stay." It tried to keep the door closed and was defeated by the party.|
|Oren Blackpenny|The tavern's owner, who bought the Coiled Coin and became bound up in the house's hunger, ownership, ledgers, and hoard.|
|Silent Coin Guardian|The final guardian that formed around the reunited Coiled Coin in the basement. The party defeated it before escaping the collapsing tavern.|
